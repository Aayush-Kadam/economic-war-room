# Economic War Room v0.9.0 — Research Preview

## What is included

- Playable seven-meeting Federal Reserve 2022 scenario
- Exact vintage PCE initialization from twelve monthly price flows
- Shared monthly Python/TypeScript engine
- Historical-policy comparison and policy-path counterfactuals
- Welfare decomposition and policy-rule benchmark system
- Reproducible official-data reconstruction
- Validated baseline of 84 Python tests and 10 browser/TypeScript tests

## Scientific status

This is a research preview, not a research-grade, causal, optimal-policy, or calibrated probabilistic forecasting system. Monetary transmission is literature-calibrated rather than locally structurally identified. The shaded range in the application is seeded simulation dispersion, not a predictive probability interval. R11 and R12 negative uncertainty results remain part of the permanent evidence record.

## Known limitations

- Twelve-month forecasting is weaker than simple baselines in the 2016–2019 evaluation.
- Policy transmission has not been structurally identified within this project.
- No tested uncertainty method passed the frozen calibration, sharpness, and proper-score gate.
- Multi-country scenarios remain experimental and outside the released scientific contract.
- Independent economist review has not yet justified a V1.0 release.
