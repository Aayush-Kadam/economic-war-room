# Validation plan

Release gates are: deterministic equality for fixed seeds; directional median responses under common shocks; finite values and bounded unemployment under extreme rates; required provenance fields; zero post-cutoff observations; full seven-decision completion; visible counterfactual labelling; TypeScript compile, production build and responsive smoke check.

Historical replay should assess direction, persistence, turning points and interval coverage—not exact fit. Robustness varies policy multiplier, Phillips slope, expectations anchoring, shock scale, neutral rate and welfare weights. A result is not robust when a qualitative policy ranking reverses over reasonable alternatives; such reversals must be shown rather than averaged away.
