# Limitations

1. The current coefficients are provisional literature-informed calibrations, not estimates from a frozen training sample. Quantitative magnitudes and welfare rankings must therefore be treated as illustrative.
2. The browser scenario uses a curated subset of official real-time indicators; it is not yet a complete ALFRED snapshot of every meeting packet.
3. Gaussian shocks understate skewness, fat tails and regime change. Financial stress is nonlinear but highly reduced-form.
4. Policy is not fully endogenous to the economy, Lucas-critique effects are not resolved, and fiscal policy is exogenous.
5. Expectations and communication are stylized; credibility is not directly observable.
6. Model uncertainty is larger than the displayed within-model fan. The band is not a frequentist confidence interval.
7. Fed 2007–09 and RBI 2022–24 are research cards only. Argentina and the synthetic generator are not released; presenting them as complete would exceed current evidence.
8. The application has not been externally peer reviewed. It is educational and not policy, legal or investment advice.
