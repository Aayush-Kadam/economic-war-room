# Country-specific extensions

The shared state-transition engine is composed with scenario extensions rather than country conditionals.

Fed 2007–09 is experimental. The module reflects the effective lower bound and separate liquidity/balance-sheet tools used during the crisis. The Federal Reserve documents lender-of-last-resort facilities, direct credit-market support and longer-term asset purchases in its [crisis response](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/bst_crisisresponse.htm).

RBI 2022–24 is experimental. The extension uses a 4% CPI target and 2–6% tolerance band, repo-rate transmission, oil/imported inflation, INR pass-through, global tightening and intervention. The mandate and target are documented by the [Reserve Bank of India](https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FS_Overview2752.aspx.html). Vintage coverage is materially weaker than for the U.S.

Neither extension is exposed as production-ready. Argentina and the synthetic generator remain out of scope.
