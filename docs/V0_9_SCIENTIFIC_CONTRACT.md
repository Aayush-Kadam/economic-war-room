# V0.9 scientific contract

Economic War Room V0.9 is a historical monetary-policy simulation research preview.

## V0.9 claims

- The seven released 2022 FOMC information packets are vintage-aware and enforce meeting-date information cutoffs.
- Fed 2022 inflation initialization uses historically reconstructed PCEPI vintages and twelve actual monthly PCE log-price changes per meeting.
- Headline CPI is supplementary and never initializes the PCE engine.
- The Python and browser monthly core implementations match within the declared numerical tolerance on six deterministic policy-and-shock fixtures.
- Counterfactual policy effects follow documented literature-calibrated player-minus-baseline response kernels.
- Official data retrieval, vintage construction, estimation, validation, and builds are reproducible from a clean clone.
- The mean PCE inflation model has documented historical validation and explicit negative results.
- Interface shading is simulation dispersion conditional on the stipulated stochastic shock process.

## V0.9 does not claim

- structural causal identification;
- optimal Federal Reserve policy or an objective ranking of policymakers;
- calibrated predictive intervals or confidence intervals;
- real-world recession, crisis, or outcome probabilities;
- universal macroeconomic forecasting superiority;
- research-grade or externally peer-reviewed validation;
- validated Fed 2007–09, RBI, Argentina, or synthetic-generator scenarios;
- that a model-generated counterfactual is observed historical truth.

## Evidence rule

The R11 and R12 uncertainty failures remain part of the release evidence. V0.9 narrows its public contract by excluding calibrated probability claims; it does not reinterpret those failed experiments as successful.
