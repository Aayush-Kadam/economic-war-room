# Data sources and provenance

Primary sources only are used in the released U.S. scenario.

- Federal Reserve Board, [FOMC calendars and statements](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm): decision dates, target ranges, official communications.
- Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, [FRED/ALFRED observations API](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/series_observations.html): series observations and explicit vintage dates.
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [CPI release archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/cpi.htm): initial CPI and core CPI releases.
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [Employment Situation archive](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/empsit.htm): unemployment and payroll releases.
- Federal Reserve Board, [June 2022 Monetary Policy Report](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/2022-06-mpr-summary.htm): contemporaneous policy context.

The machine-readable registry records institution, units, transformation, frequency, vintage requirement and URL. Redistribution terms are respected by distributing retrieval code and metadata rather than bulk source data.
